Overview

The Brief

A bright, boutique-feeling corporate gifting site for two markets on day one: English for the UAE, native Arabic for KSA. Phase 1 is deliberately light — a browsable product catalog feeding a simple "request a quote" flow to your sales team, with no checkout, no payment processing, and no account system. Phase 2 adds full e-commerce on top of this same foundation, so nothing built now gets thrown away later.

Scope of Work

Design
Figma UI/UX Design
Fully mobile responsive across all pages. Bright, boutique retail aesthetic — white/off-white base, lifestyle imagery, spacious navigation. Designed once in a single language, with the Arabic layout applied during development.

UAE (English) and KSA (Arabic) experiences on one store, with a region and language dropdown in the site header that visitors can switch from at any time. Category pages include simple drawer filters; product pages show images, specs, and product code.

Every page below is designed and built, and every one is fully mobile responsive:

  • Homepage with visual category grid
  • 7 main category landing pages
  • 40 subcategory listing pages with drawer filters
  • Product detail page template (images, specs, product code, Add to Quote)
  • Quote request / enquiry page
  • About Us
  • Contact Us
  • Standard policy pages (Privacy Policy, Terms)
  • 404 / not found page

Your finalized taxonomy covers 7 main categories, 40 subcategories, and 274 products total: Apparel (5 subcategories, 38 products), Drinkware (4, 34), Bags & Luggage (5, 40), Trade Shows & Events (4, 32), Office (6, 45), Tech (11, 60), and National Day/Festival/Seasonal (5, 25).

You'll provide the product images and descriptions (referenced from Mid Ocean), so our team isn't sourcing or selecting products independently. We handle formatting, bilingual entry, and upload.

So the build isn't held up waiting on content, we'll use placeholder text and images throughout development. Your final content is added as you supply it, and any content that arrives after handover can be added by your team using the video guides we provide.

To keep the timeline tight, we design each page once, in a single language, and get your approval on that. The Arabic layout and content are then applied during development, where the same approved design is mirrored for right-to-left reading. This avoids designing every screen twice and gets us into build faster.

Visitors choose their region and language from a dropdown in the site header (UAE / English or KSA / Arabic), available on every page and switchable at any time.

We've deliberately avoided forcing an automatic redirect based on location. Location doesn't reliably indicate language preference in the Gulf, and forced redirects can interfere with how search engines crawl and index your pages, which would work against the SEO goals for this build.

No purchase happens on the site in Phase 1. A buyer selects a product and quantity, submits their contact details, and that request lands with your sales team to follow up on directly, by phone, email, or WhatsApp.

Live chat is set up using Tawk.to, a free tool with no monthly subscription, staffed by your own team. It supports your 8am–8pm hours with offline message capture outside them, and a direct WhatsApp link gives buyers a 24/7 channel.

One thing to be clear about: automatic English–Arabic translation inside live chat is not included, and isn't available without a paid third-party tool. Incoming Arabic messages need to be read and replied to by a person. WhatsApp does let your team read an incoming Arabic message in English for free, built into the app, but replies still need either an Arabic-speaking team member or a quick manual translation before sending.

Section 03

What's Not Included

  • E-commerce checkout, cart, and payment gateway integration — reserved for Phase 2
  • Minimum order quantity logic, tiered bulk pricing, and company/B2B accounts — Phase 2
  • Custom branding options on quote requests (screen printing, UV, laser engraving, embossing, embroidery) or artwork upload
  • AI chatbot — Phase 1 support is human-staffed only
  • Off-page SEO, backlink building, or PR — a separate ongoing service, not part of the site build
  • A compiled, branded e-catalogue (flipbook/downloadable PDF combining supplier content) — a separate project, to be scoped and quoted once Phase 1 is live
  • Paid live-chat or translation subscriptions — Phase 1 uses free native tools plus manual translation where needed
